# The first 30 minutes

**DESIGNED AND IMPLEMENTED (M8). NOT TESTED by a person:** the times below are estimates from the scripted play and
the quest costs, not from a playtest.

The opening should feel like arriving somewhere alive, not like reading a manual. Each mechanic appears when it is
needed, is taught by doing it once, and pays back with something the player can see.

| Time | What happens | What teaches it | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | WELCOME, choose a Carrier on animated cards, name the island (or not) | the title | a world that is yours |
| 0:30 | The intro: the Carrier surfaces bare, land forms, the Blobbi teleports in, says hello | the cinematic | (skippable at any moment) |
| 1:00 | First tip: walk. Goal box: *Meet your Blobbi* | tip, quest | friendship, a journal note |
| 2:00 | *Explore the island*: look at three things; sticks and stones glint | tip "pick something up" | a journal note |
| 4:00 | *Gather supplies* (5 things), the bag | tips "pick up", "open the bag" | seeds |
| 6:00 | *Your first tool*: the axe in CRAFT | tip "craft" | fence plans |
| 8:00 | *Gather wood*: three hits, a tree falls, its stump will regrow | tip "tool" | stones |
| 10:00 | *Building materials*: 6 wood, 3 stones (one more tree) | the quest's hint | ready to build |
| 12:00 | *Build your first home*: the green/red preview, the shelter rising | tip "build" | seeds, furniture plans |
| 14:00 | *Step inside*: a warm room, a window on the sea | tip "enter" | a potted plant (gift) |
| 15:00 | *Make it yours*: place the plant | tip "furnish" | a cozier home |
| 16:00 | *Prepare a farm plot* → *Plant* → *Water* | tip "farm" | carrot and radish seeds, a watering can |
| 22:00 | Crops grow while watered; the island's first request appears after the shelter | goal box, journal | small gifts |
| 26:00 | *Harvest food* → *Feed Blobbi* | the quest hints | pumpkin and potato seeds, lantern and bench plans |
| 28:00 | Tip: the island can grow (GROWTH shows what it needs) | tip "grow" | a goal for the next sessions |
| 30:00+ | *Make it home* (furniture at a workbench), cooking, the Carrier's hum, then a Cottage | quests, tips "cook", "carrier", "home" | new rooms and styles |

Principles kept:

- **One thing at a time.** One tip, one main goal; side quests wait in the journal.
- **No chores.** Costs stay small (the first home is 6 wood and 3 stones, 2–3 trees); everything regrows.
- **Never blocked.** Tips never pause the game; the island can be explored freely from the first second.
- **The island can grow.** The idea is planted by the Carrier's own presence (the intro), the GROWTH tip after the
  first harvest, and house upgrades that ask for the island's harmony.
- **Same adventure on every Carrier.** All quests are reachable on turtle, manta and whale islands
  (`tests/first-home.test.ts`, `tests/new-world-start.test.ts`).
