# M11D.1: Baby Blobbi visual support

**Status: IMPLEMENTED, AWAITING MANUAL VALIDATION.** Branch `develop`, starting point `f28d0c5` (M11C.5 docs), save
schema 12 (**unchanged**). Nobody has used any of this yet: the manual tests below (BABY-01..12, INTROFIX-01..03) are
all NOT TESTED. Automated checks are not approval. The default starting Blobbi is unchanged (the demo adult
`blobbi-9`); no key generated, no event published, no canonical identity rule, energy or evolution changed.

## What was implemented

| Area | What | Code |
|---|---|---|
| Baby renderer (V3) | The kit's own baby of the same genome (`renderBlobbiSvg(genome, { stage: 'baby' })`): droplet body, simple eyes, budding traits, flatter baby paint, no arms or feet, rasterized with the pixel rules. A stage travels with the pixel identity (`PixelBlobbiIdentity.stage`), so the world sprite, the native camera sprites, the hand rig, the HUD portrait and MY BLOBBIS all draw the same Baby. | `src/blobbi/canonical.ts` (`stage` option, `BABY_HEIGHT_RATIO`, `babyHeightFor`, `babyHopFor`), `src/blobbi/identity.ts` (`stagedIdentity`), `src/blobbi/sprite.ts` |
| Rasterizer | Three additions, none of which the adult uses (adult pixels are byte-identical, pinned by `tests/__snapshots__/blobbi-hashes.json`): a stroke may name a gradient (the baby mouth: drawn in its middle colour); a gradient-filled eye white takes its light side, and a **simple eye** (a pupil without an iris) is drawn by the iris rule; `insideBody` keeps the baby's half-transparent cheeks inside the droplet (outside it they became outlined nubs that read as little arms). | `src/blobbi/svg-raster.ts` |
| Size | Drawn at its adult self's pixels per kit unit, so it stands the kit's two thirds (0.655 of the adult, measured) at every camera size, with a 10 px floor so the 12 px overview Baby keeps a face (12 → 10, 24 → 16, 48 → 31, 72 → 47 px). The **collision footprint stays the adult's** of the same genome: a Baby walks, reaches and pathfinds exactly like its adult self, so gameplay is identical. | `canonical.ts`, `src/sim/live-world.ts` (`makeBlobbi`) |
| Hop | The kit's hop gait lifts the body 9.6 kit units on four of six walk frames (under a pixel at play sizes: it would glide). The pixel Baby keeps that rhythm (lifted on frames 1, 2, 4, 5; landing on 0 and 3) with a readable whole-pixel lift: a twelfth of its height, at least 1 px (1 px at 10 to 16 px, 3 px at close range, 4 px up close). The ground point and the world's shadow do not move. | `babyHopFor` |
| Expressions | The kit's baby faces: no brows, lids and a shallower mouth carry happy, sad, surprised, sleepy; blinks and asleep as for adults; the shared whole-pixel gaze. | kit + raster |
| Tools ("invisible arms", approved) | No arm is drawn. The grip sits one pixel outside the droplet at its widest lower row: the side the adult's hand would be from the front and back; from the side it hangs at the **back edge** (never across the face) and the swing carries it to the **front edge** for the blow, the cast and the held rod. The far hand stays hidden at rest, as for adults. Tools are sized to the Baby (about two thirds of the adult's). Measured on the drawn sprite, so the tool rides every hop frame. All five tools (axe, pickaxe, hoe, watering can, fishing rod) and their copper versions; the rod keeps its line tip. Gameplay is unchanged (the same actions, reach and timings). | `src/game/equipment.ts` (`babyHand`, `attachTool`) |
| V1 / V2 Babies | The kit's one authored baby drawing (V2 uses it too: "Baby V2 is not designed yet"), in the event's own three colours, rasterized: `pixelDialect` rewrites the authored file (CSS stop styles, `white`, `rgba`, no part names) into the rasterizer's dialect shape for shape, regrouping the eyes, and refuses anything it does not recognise. Front and back only (the kit draws the front for a side facing too), open or asleep, no expressions; the same whole-pixel hop. Loaded lazily (its own 230 kB chunk; only MY BLOBBIS needs it). | `src/blobbi/v1-baby.ts` |
| MY BLOBBIS | A **V3 Baby may visit** ("A Baby, ready to visit your island and help with every tool."), previewed as the Baby that will walk; choosing stays explicit (connecting never changes who walks). A V1/V2 Baby shows its real picture with "comes from an earlier generation (V1). Here is its picture, but it cannot visit yet". Eggs: "Eggs cannot visit this world yet; once it hatches, the Baby can." V1/V2 adults and unknown generations unchanged (colours only). If a chosen Baby has grown up in another app, the next read of the list draws it as the adult. EN, PT-BR, ES, no em dash. | `src/nostr/blobbis.ts`, `src/ui/screens/blobbis.ts`, `src/i18n/messages/*` |

## Generation compatibility

| Blobbi | Drawn | Visits the island | Faithful? |
|---|---|---|---|
| V3 adult | the kit's adult, gene for gene | yes (since M11C) | yes |
| **V3 baby** | the kit's baby of the same genome | **yes (M11D.1)** | yes: same seed, colours, markings, budding traits; pixel deviations listed below |
| V3 egg | its colours | no (an egg cannot walk) | n/a |
| **V1 baby** | the kit's V1 baby art in its event colours | **no (picture only)** | the picture is the kit's own; the art has no side view, no walk rig and no expressions, so the pixel world cannot animate it as a V3 Baby |
| **V2 baby** | the same V1 art (as the kit draws it) | **no (picture only)** | as V1; the kit has no V2 baby design |
| V1 / V2 adult | colours only | no | unchanged since M11C (their world form is a separate proposal, [generations](../architecture/BLOBBI-GENERATION-COMPATIBILITY.md) §5) |
| unknown generation | nothing | no | never guessed |

Nothing ever draws an unrelated V3 Blobbi in place of a V1/V2 one.

**Pixel deviations from the kit's baby (deliberate, documented):** the cheeks are kept inside the droplet (the kit's
half-transparent ovals overhang its narrow body: in pixels they read as arms); the hop is a whole-pixel lift (the
kit's is sub-pixel); a 10 px minimum height at the 12 px overview (the kit's proportion would give 8 px, no face);
the mouth is one colour (the kit's 3-stop gradient across a 1-pixel line).

## Intro animation investigation

**Report:** on an existing Brave profile the Carrier's emergence starts too far along or finishes too quickly; a new
game in another browser plays normally.

| Possible cause | Finding |
|---|---|
| Animation timing | **Not the cause.** The intro's clock is real frame time, each frame capped at 0.1 s (`IntroCinematic.frame`), reset when an intro starts (`lastFrameTime = 0`). A slow tab or a stalled first frame plays it slower, never faster; a long-open tab never skips ahead (tests). |
| Existing save, resume logic, intro flags | **Not the cause.** A new island's intro always starts at 0; the save stores only `intro: { state, at: 0 }` (pending/done/skipped), never a position. An existing save of another island changes nothing (tested: identical elapsed time and duration). Continue never replays a seen intro; New Game always plays it. |
| Cached assets | **Not the cause.** The intro is drawn procedurally; there are no image or timing assets to cache. |
| **Browser storage: the reduced-motion setting** | **The likely cause.** The reduced-motion intro (`reducedTiming`) matches the symptom exactly: the Carrier's shadow is already in on the first frame (full: it rises from 0.8 s), and the surfacing takes 1 s (full: 4 s); the whole intro is 19.5 s instead of about 32. It is chosen only by the `reducedMotion` setting, stored per browser profile in `blobbi-pixel-lab:settings`. That record exists in the Brave profile's storage for the dev server (checked read-only; its value could not be read, and further inspection of the browser profile was not permitted). |
| **Bug found (fixed)** | The settings record stored `reducedMotion` whenever ANY setting changed, so the system's reduced-motion value at that moment (or one toggle made while testing) was frozen silently for that profile. Now the stored value counts only when the player toggled REDUCED MOTION (`reducedMotionChosen: true`); otherwise the game follows the system's `prefers-reduced-motion`. Records written before M11D.1 keep their stored value (it may be a real accessibility choice; an update never drops one). No save is touched; New Game and Continue are unchanged. |

**For the owner (Brave):** open SETTINGS in the game and look at REDUCED MOTION. If it says ON, turn it OFF (that
records your choice) and start a new island: the full emergence should play. Alternatively, in Brave's developer
console on the game's page: `localStorage.getItem('blobbi-pixel-lab:settings')`. If it is OFF and the intro is still
short, that is a new finding: please note the browser, its version, the screen's refresh rate and whether the tab was
in the background.

## Known limitations

- **KI-76** V1/V2 Babies cannot visit (front/back art only, no rig); shown with their real picture.
- **KI-77** The Baby is the kit's proportion: small at the 12 px overview (10 px) and at exploration (16 px); faces read
  from 16 px. The owner may prefer a larger Baby (one constant, `BABY_MIN_HEIGHT` / the ratio).
- **KI-78** Beds, the canoe, the Carrier's listening spot and the teleport use the adult's anchors: the Baby sits where
  the adult would (visually fine in the screenshots; not reviewed at every bed and boat pose).
- **KI-79** A Baby's work, stats and needs are the adult's (no 4-segment bars, no smaller energy pool): by design for
  this milestone (energy changes were out of scope).
- **KI-80** A pre-M11D.1 settings record that froze reduced motion ON still plays the short intro until the player
  turns it off in SETTINGS (kept on purpose, see above).
- Particles (sparkles, the "!" mark) are placed for an adult's height above a Baby.

## Decisions requiring the owner's approval

1. **Baby size**: the kit's proportion (two thirds of the adult, 16 px at exploration) or a larger pixel Baby?
2. **V1/V2 Babies visiting**: allowed with the front/back art only (the side shows the front, as the kit itself does),
   or wait for a V1/V2 world form?
3. **The side carry** (back edge at rest, front edge for the blow), approved in principle as "invisible arms": confirm
   after playing.
4. **Baby stats and energy** (4 segments, a smaller work-energy pool): still the M11C.5 proposal, not implemented.
5. **The guest's own Baby** and its identity (M11C.5 decision 1) remain open: the default starting Blobbi is unchanged.
