## 4. Part D: seamless entry, by scenario

Once per page load, at the first start of play, `autoEnter` (`main.ts`) asks `decideEntry` (`src/game/world-entry.ts`).

| Scenario | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1. First-time visitor | The title (WELCOME), guest first, the birth cinematic on NEW ADVENTURE: unchanged |
| 2. Returning connected player, one island of theirs | Straight into the island with their Blobbi (the teleport arrival; no intro again) |
| 3. Connected, no island on this device, a current Blobbi that can walk | The title's WELCOME BACK page: "NEW ADVENTURE WITH PUDDING" (no new egg), MY BLOBBIS, BACK. Says islands stay on the device where they were made |
| 4. Offline (relays silent) | Straight in after the read gives up (4 s; about 7 s from page load in the browser check), with the Blobbi of this device |
| 5. From another Blobbi app | Only what is safe today: if this browser already has a session, scenario 2 or 3; else the title and its CONNECT / I HAVE A SECRET BACKUP. Nothing is signed in from a link (BLOBBI-ACCOUNT §2) |
| 6. Another current Blobbi (chosen in another app) | "WHO COMES ALONG?": BRING MOCHI (selected, then in; the island stays as it is) or CONTINUE WITH PUDDING (remembered for that island and that current Blobbi: not asked again until it changes) |
| 7. The title on purpose | SETTINGS > MAIN MENU (kept across reloads until play starts again), `#menu` in the link, or any key or tap on the title before the entry settles. Not repeated after returning to the menu |
| Several islands of theirs | The islands page: the player chooses |
| Islands another identity or a guest played last | The title (never entered for this identity) |
| Disconnected | The title, every time (the account record is gone) |

### 4.1 Fix: the intermittent main menu (found in manual testing)

**Symptom.** A returning connected player with a saved island sometimes entered it on reload and sometimes stopped on
the main menu, alternating, with hard and normal refreshes alike.

**Root cause.** Not the relays. While an island is played, the game writes it into the address bar itself
(`saveHash`: `#seed=...&mode=play&pubkey=...&d=...&kind=...`). `autoEnter` refused any address with `seed`, treating
it as a link from someone else, so a reload from inside the island kept the title. That page load then wrote the plain
`#mode=play` back, so the next reload, from the title, went in. Reproduced in the real page: 5 of 10 reloads on the
title, every one of them from inside the island, with fast, slow and offline relays alike.

**Fix.** `entryAddress` (`src/game/world-entry.ts`) keeps only `#menu` (and the lab's `#intro`) as "keep the title".
An island named by the address is handed to `decideEntry` as `linked`: it is resumed only when it is saved on this
device, and it is then the only candidate, with the same checks (its last visitor and the walking Blobbi are this
identity's; another current Blobbi still asks who comes along). A link to an island not saved here keeps the title,
which offers to play it, as before. The waits run through `runEntry`, in a fixed order (the account, its presence,
then the current Blobbi), stopping after each wait if the player led or the account changed. SETTINGS > MAIN MENU
now also writes `menu` into the address until play starts again, so reloads after choosing the main menu stay on it
(before, they kept the title only by the same accident).

**Why entry is now deterministic.** The decision depends only on what this device keeps (the account record, the vault,
the saves, `island-visits`, the selection) and the read's answer, never on which address the previous page left. The
read always answers within 4 s (a timeout, an offline relay and a missing profile all lead to the device's Blobbi), so
relay timing changes only how long the title shows, never where the player lands.



| Kind of identity | Where it lives | How it is told apart |
|---|---|---|
| Guest identity (a Blobbi met here) | Key in the IndexedDB vault (AES-GCM, non-extractable wrapping key); public record `blobbi-pixel-lab:guest-blobbis` | `ownerKind: 'guest'`; the account stays guest until a real share or a restore |
| Shared (registered) | Same, plus a finished LIVE activation run | `activatedAt` with a live run; a rehearsal is `practicedAt` (M11D.6) |
| Restored | Vault record `origin: 'restored'` | Adopted as the account (`method: 'vault'`) unless another account is connected (M11D.7) |
| Browser extension (NIP-07) | Only the public key, `blobbi-pixel-lab:account` | `method: 'nip07'`; "present" only while the extension is |
| Disconnected | No account record | The vault keeps its keys (backups still restore); nothing enters automatically |
| Local adventures | One save per island (world identity), never owned by a Blobbi | The last visitor (`island-visits`) links an island to an identity's Blobbi |

Seamless entry cannot: make a keypair (it never calls `reserve`), hatch a Blobbi (it only enters saved islands or
selects an existing Blobbi of the account through MY BLOBBIS's own chooser), switch accounts (it reads the snapshot
and stops if it changed meanwhile), load another identity's island (only islands its Blobbis played last), overwrite an
island (entering is CONTINUE), reuse a session after disconnect (no record, no entry), or publish (no publisher or
signer is reachable from it; the browser check counted zero EVENT frames).

## 7. Manual validation checklist (disposable identities only; never paste a key into chat or a terminal)

Use a fresh browser profile and a production build on its own port (not 5240 or 5244).

1. **Returning connected player.** Restore a disposable identity with a shared Blobbi, make an island with it, play a
   minute, reload. Expected: straight into the island with that Blobbi, no title stop, no intro.
2. **Automatic entry with the current Blobbi.** In Standalone, that identity's current Blobbi is the same. Reload
   Pixel: no question.
3. **Another browser, no island.** Restore the same identity in a second fresh profile, close the restore page with
   NOT NOW, reload. Expected: WELCOME BACK with the Blobbi's name; NEW ADVENTURE WITH <NAME>; no egg hatches.
4. **Switch in another app.** In Standalone, choose another Blobbi of the same identity (it publishes kind 11125).
5. **Back to Pixel after the switch.** Reload the first browser. Expected: WHO COMES ALONG? Try CONTINUE WITH (then
   reload: not asked again), then clear by switching again in Standalone and try BRING.
6. **Offline with a save.** Turn the network off, reload. Expected: into the island after a few seconds, same Blobbi.
7. **Title on purpose.** SETTINGS > MAIN MENU; reload with `#menu`; or press a key on the title right after loading.
8. **Disconnect and reconnect.** SETTINGS > ACCOUNT > DISCONNECT, reload: the title. Reconnect with I HAVE A SECRET
   BACKUP (the identity is already on the device: nothing changes but the account): reload: straight in again.
9. **No duplicate publishing.** Several reloads: nothing is sent (the identity and Blobbi were shared before; Pixel
   publishes only on SHARE with consent).
10. **Same identity in both apps.** SETTINGS > ACCOUNT > ADVANCED DETAILS npub matches Standalone's; the Blobbi's look
    and name match in both.

## 8. Limitations

- Standalone's per-device pick is not visible to Pixel; "current" is the profile's.
- An egg as the current Blobbi is never offered (eggs do not walk); the player picks from MY BLOBBIS.
- With a NIP-07 account, "present" means the extension is installed; its public key is not asked again on load (no
  prompt), so a switched extension account is noticed only on the next CONNECT. Seamless entry only reads.
- The read waits up to 4 s on the title; the title is shown meanwhile and any key gives the player the lead. Entering
  first and asking "who comes along?" later was not done: the question belongs before the island opens.
- With several islands, a reload from inside one goes back to that one (its address names it); a plain load shows the
  islands page.
- No cross-app sign-in (BLOBBI-ACCOUNT §2).
