## 2. Root causes

| Issue | Root cause (confirmed in the code) |
|---|---|
| 1. The main menu showed, then left by itself | `setMode('play')` reset the title and started `autoEnter` behind it; the run (vault or extension check, then the relays, up to 4 s) ran while the menu was drawn and took input. By design in M11D.8 ("the title is shown meanwhile"), wrong in practice. |
| 2. CONNECT (NIP-07), then a Blobbi chosen in MY BLOBBIS: nothing advanced | `BlobbisScreen.choose` only recorded the choice and said "X is chosen"; the list stayed open, and closing it left the title on the page it was on (LOGIN WITH NOSTR, then the main menu with CONTINUE ADVENTURE). The automatic entry runs once per page load, so connecting on the title never re-evaluated anything. No state bug: a missing transition. |
| 3. Boca rosa, selected in Ditto, was not recognised | Ditto does not publish a selection. Picking a Blobbi on its page (`handleSelectBlobbi`) writes only Ditto's own `localStorage` key `blobbi:selected:d:<pubkey>`; kind 11125 `current_companion` is published only by its explicit **Set as companion** button, and pressing that button on the current companion UNSETS it. Details and evidence: [CURRENT-BLOBBI §4](../architecture/CURRENT-BLOBBI.md). A second, Pixel-side cause for the missing automatic entry: with a NIP-07 account, "present" was `window.nostr` existing at that instant; an extension that injects late read as absent, and nothing waited for it. |
| 4. A switched extension account was not noticed | `present` never asked the extension for its key; the remembered public key was trusted on every load (M11D.8 §8 said so). |

Not verified with real events: whether the owner's real Ditto profile names Boca rosa (that needs the owner's identity,
which automated checks never use). The check is in §7.

## 3. Issue 1: the world-entry loading screen (`src/ui/entry-screen.ts`)

- Shown by `autoEnter` only while a CONNECTED account is really being looked at: `entry.begin()` before the run,
  `entry.end()` the moment it settles. A guest, `#menu`, SETTINGS > MAIN MENU, a lab mode: the title at once, no page.
- "Your Blobbi is on the way!" / "Checking your adventures and looking for your Blobbi across connected worlds..."
  (English, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish: `entry.*` keys). Three little worlds joined by travelling lights (the
  recovery animation, `drawConnectedWorlds(..., { sparks: 3 })`), the walking Blobbi arriving on the middle one as a
  soft silhouette (drifts down and fades in over the first 0.8 s, then breathes). With reduced motion: still, whole.
- No minimum time: a quick entry ends the page before any button appears. MAIN MENU appears after 1.5 s; ESC (or
  Backspace) works at any time; ordinary keys are swallowed (they never reach the menu behind, never cancel). After 4 s
  the page says other worlds are slow; after 15 s it gives up by itself and the title comes back. With a NIP-07 account
  it says "If your browser extension asks, confirm there." while the extension is asked.
- `titleFrame` draws the page instead of the menu while it is up (`if (entry.active) entry.render(...)`); the keyboard
  and the pointer reach it first. The menu is never drawn under a pending entry.
- Offline: unchanged from M11D.8 (the relays give up after 4 s, then straight in with the Blobbi of this device).

## 4. Issue 2: the chosen Blobbi goes on (`decideChosen`, `blobbisScreen.onChosen`)

After a successful choice in MY BLOBBIS over the title, the list closes and `decideChosen` (`src/game/world-entry.ts`)
decides from this device alone (the player just chose: it is the current one here): exactly one island whose last
visitor is this identity's: straight in; several: the islands page; none (or only islands others played): WELCOME
BACK with "NEW ADVENTURE WITH <NAME>" (the Carrier choice; no new egg), and, when islands are saved here, an ISLANDS
row and a body line saying so (`title.arrived.bodyIslands`). During play (MY BLOBBIS from the HUD) nothing more
happens. Nothing is made, hatched or overwritten: the same `carryOut` as the automatic entry, which only enters saved
islands (CONTINUE) or opens title pages.

## 5. Issue 4: the extension's account is checked (`NostrAccount.verifyExtension`)

- Once per page load, for a NIP-07 account: one `getPublicKey` (the same question CONNECT asks; the extension's own
  prompt may appear the first time an origin asks, and nos2x, Alby and the like remember the answer per site), with a
  6 s limit. The remembered record is NEVER changed by the answer. Results (`AccountSnapshot.extension`): `verified`
  (the same key), `mismatch` (another key: the player switched accounts in the extension), `unconfirmed` (no answer
  in time, a refusal, a locked extension), `absent` (no extension), `unchecked` (a guest or a vault identity).
- The automatic entry treats only `verified` as "present": with any other answer the title shows (never the remembered
  account's island). The title's connected row then says "ANOTHER ACCOUNT IN THE EXTENSION", the LOGIN page and the
  ACCOUNT page say it in a sentence, and CONNECT / CHECK THE ACCOUNT AGAIN (explicit) is the only way to switch (the
  existing account switching: the other account starts fresh, the first one's Blobbi no longer walks).
- The signer still has no way to sign from here; registration through the extension already refuses an event signed by
  another key (`activation.ts`, `wrong-signer`). No private key is ever seen.

## 6. Issue 3: the current Blobbi from Ditto

Pixel's resolver is unchanged and correct for Ditto's events: Ditto's profile is kind 11125 with the kit's canonical
`d` (`blobbonaut-<pubkey12>`), `b` marker and a bare `current_companion` d, by the player, on relays Pixel reads
(`relay.ditto.pub`, `relay.dreamith.to`, `relay.primal.net` are in both lists). `tests/m11d8-2-ditto.test.ts` feeds
Pixel events shaped exactly as Ditto builds them (the kit's `buildBlobbonautTags` + `updateBlobbonautTags`, signed by
a disposable key): the companion set in Ditto is resolved; a page selection alone (no tag) is `no-companion`; an unset
is `no-companion`; the newest profile wins; a companion the relays did not return is `missing`; another author or a
forged tag is ignored. No replacement protocol, no event from Pixel, kinds 11125 and 31124 untouched.

What changed on Pixel's side for the owner's case: the extension is now waited for and verified (§5), so a NIP-07
account enters automatically when the extension answers with its key.

## 7. Manual validation checklist (disposable identities only; never paste a key into chat or a terminal)

Use a fresh browser profile and a production build on its own port (not 5240 or 5244).

1. **Loading page.** With a saved island and a connected identity, reload: "Your Blobbi is on the way!" (no menu),
   then the island. Throttle the network (DevTools, Slow 3G): the page stays, MAIN MENU appears after about 1.5 s, the
   slow line after 4 s; ESC goes to the main menu and the island does not open by itself afterwards.
2. **Quick entry.** With a fast network: no button flashes; the island opens within about a second.
3. **NIP-07, choose a Blobbi.** Fresh profile, CONNECT with the extension, MY BLOBBIS, BRING one: with no island here,
   WELCOME BACK with "NEW ADVENTURE WITH <NAME>" and the Carrier choice; make an island; reload: the loading page, then
   straight in. With one island already theirs: straight in from BRING.
4. **Ditto's companion.** In Ditto, open the Blobbi and press **Set as companion** (a page selection is not enough);
   reload Pixel: it is offered ("who comes along?") or walks in. Press the button again in Ditto (unset): nothing is
   offered.
5. **Extension switched.** In the extension, switch to another account; reload Pixel: the title, "ANOTHER ACCOUNT IN
   THE EXTENSION" on the connected row, the sentence on LOGIN and ACCOUNT pages; the island is not opened. CONNECT
   switches explicitly; DISCONNECT then CONNECT works too.
6. **Extension locked or absent.** Lock it (or disable it) and reload: the title, the sentence on the ACCOUNT page;
   nothing opens by itself; CONTINUE still works.
7. **Offline.** Network off, reload: the loading page, then the island after a few seconds.
8. **No publishing.** Nothing is sent on any of these loads (the extension never asks to sign).

## 10. Limitations

- An extension that asks every time (no remembered permission) shows its prompt on every page load; the page says to
  confirm there, and after 6 s the title comes back with "did not confirm" (CHECK THE ACCOUNT AGAIN asks once more).
- Ditto's page selection stays invisible to every other app (its own storage); only **Set as companion** is shared.
  Not worked around, not replaced (CURRENT-BLOBBI §3 lists it for the ecosystem).
- Whether the owner's real profile names Boca rosa was not checked (no real identity in automated checks); §7 item 4.
- The loading page is drawn at the title's scale (whole pixels): on narrow phones its text is small, as the title's.
