# M11D.3: main menu, several islands, the pixel egg and the first birth

**Status: IMPLEMENTED, AWAITING MANUAL VALIDATION (local commits, not pushed).** Branch `develop`, starting point
`804210f` (M11D.2 docs; `28b2cf9` and `804210f` local, two ahead of `origin/develop`, preserved), island save schema
12 (**unchanged**). Nobody has played any of this yet: the manual tests below (MENU-01..08, ISLE-01..07, BIRTH-01..08,
VISIT-01..04) are all NOT TESTED. Automated checks are not approval. No key generated, no event published, no
canonical identity rule or lifecycle stage changed; V1/V2 world forms are untouched.

## 1. Main menu (IMPLEMENTED)

| Situation | Rows |
|---|---|
| No saved island (first time) | **NEW ADVENTURE** ("Meet your Carrier and your Blobbi") · LOGIN WITH NOSTR ("Optional: bring your own Blobbis") · SETTINGS · about |
| Islands saved | **CONTINUE ADVENTURE** (one island: plays it; several: "Choose one of your N islands", the island list) · NEW ADVENTURE · LOGIN WITH NOSTR (connected: "YOUR NOSTR ACCOUNT, connected as …") · SETTINGS · islands · about |

- **NEW ADVENTURE** with islands saved first shows the narrative confirmation "A new adventure?" (EN, the briefed
  PT-BR, ES): "Your island is still waiting for you. If you start another adventure, your Carrier will keep sailing the
  ocean until you come back. Do you really want to set off?" START A NEW ADVENTURE ("Your islands stay saved") or BACK
  TO MY ISLAND ("Nothing changes"). It deletes nothing and implies no deletion (tested).
- Then the Carrier, the name, and, only when the player already has a Blobbi worth keeping (not the default visitor),
  **"Who will live here?"**: MEET A NEW BLOBBI ("An egg hatches on your island") or CONTINUE WITH <name>.
- **SETTINGS > MAIN MENU (your island is saved)** while playing: the island is saved, play stops, the title returns.
  Shown only in play (never on the title itself).
- The language button and every existing title page (islands, delete with its confirmation, about, intro previews)
  stay as they were.

## 2. Several islands (IMPLEMENTED)

- **Identity**: an island's stable unique identifier is its existing world identity (the generation parameters,
  `seed=…|kind=…|…`), the key of its save `blobbi-pixel-lab:save:v1:<identity>`. NEW ADVENTURE never reuses one that
  has a save (`newWorldSeeds`, tested).
- **Cards** (CONTINUE ADVENTURE with several islands, or ISLANDS): the island's name, ACTIVE, its Carrier, day, quests
  and buildings (as before), and a new line: when it was last played ("Played today", "Played yesterday", "Played N
  days ago") and the Blobbi that came last ("with Mochi", "with your Baby Blobbi"), from local memory.
- **No invented ownership**: an island never belongs to a Blobbi or an account; an account may play any number of
  islands, a Blobbi may visit any number of them; connecting or disconnecting never touches islands.

## 3. Save migration: none was needed (zero-write, VERIFIED)

The audit found that islands were **already one record per island** (since M2.1 every save is keyed by its world
identity); "one island" was only a product rule of the M10 phase C title (`ACTIVE_WORLD_KEY`, NEW ISLAND hidden
unless `#islands=many`). So the multi-island menu reads the existing records as they are:

- no record is rewritten, renamed, converted or deleted; the schema stays 12; the active-island key and the
  last-played index keep their meaning (CONTINUE with one island; the ACTIVE mark);
- idempotent and interruption-safe by construction (reading writes nothing; tested: storage byte-identical after
  listing, however often);
- recoverable: unreadable or newer records keep the M10 phase C protection (copied aside, never overwritten);
- progress, Carrier type, buildings, inventory, quests, world state and settings are therefore untouched.

New, separate per-browser records (never inside an island save): `blobbi-pixel-lab:local-blobbis` (§6) and
`blobbi-pixel-lab:island-visits` (§9).

## 4. Save protection

- NEW ADVENTURE never overwrites: a new island always gets an identity without a save; with islands saved it asks
  first, in words that promise the island keeps waiting (true: nothing is deleted).
- A creation that fails (storage full or refused) leaves every existing island exactly as it was (tested), and an
  egg made for it is forgotten.
- Permanent deletion is unchanged (ISLANDS, a separate page, KEEP IT by default, DELETE FOREVER only after a pause)
  and deletes only that island's save: never an account, a key or a Blobbi.

## 5. Login with Nostr (IMPLEMENTED, optional, read-only)

The existing NIP-07 connection (M11B), nothing new: no secret, no signing, no publishing. The login page explains
Nostr in plain words ("a free, open way to keep your Blobbis with you across games and apps. It is optional"); without
an extension it says so ("You can keep playing as a guest"). Not connected: CONNECT · PLAY AS A GUEST. Connected:
"Welcome, <name>! Do you want to continue with one of your Blobbis, or meet a new one?":

- **CONTINUE WITH ONE OF MY BLOBBIS** opens MY BLOBBIS over the title: eligible Blobbis, explicit choice, the V1/V2
  world-form explanation and consent (M11D.2) exactly as in play, canonical identity untouched, nothing published.
- **MEET A NEW BLOBBI** ("Born here; not on your account yet") starts a new adventure in which a local Blobbi hatches.
  No canonical Blobbi event is created, and nothing promises account persistence (the reserved identity and
  registration are not built).

## 6. Local Blobbis (IMPLEMENTED; a local presentation)

MEET A NEW BLOBBI creates a **local Blobbi**: the seed `local-blobbi-<16 hex>` (8 random bytes from
`crypto.getRandomValues`: a name, not a key), drawn with the kit's procedural genome, born as a Baby from its egg on the
island it was met on. It is chosen in this browser (`stage: 'baby'` kept without an account for local Blobbis only),
walks every island the player opens, and survives accounts connecting and disconnecting. **It is not a canonical V3
Blobbi**: its look is not derived from an owner key and d, it has no address, no event. When reserved guest
identities arrive ([plan](../architecture/GUEST-IDENTITY-PLAN.md)), a local Blobbi cannot be registered with the same
look: a decision for the owner (see §14).

## 7. The pixel egg (IMPLEMENTED)

`src/blobbi/egg.ts`: **the kit's own V3 egg** of the Blobbi's genome (`renderBlobbiSvg(genome, { stage: 'egg',
eggCrack })`: its shell shape, lean, spots, accent spot, highlight, patch and colours from the genome's egg genes),
rasterized with the same pixel rules; the crack group's stroke attributes rewritten into the rasterizer's dialect.
No face, no limbs. About 0.56 of its adult's height, at least 9 px; crisp at every camera size (drawn natively).

- **Rocking**: the egg never turns; it rocks on its base: the drawing is rotated about its ground point before
  rasterizing (whole degrees, cached) and rolls a pixel along its base. Gentle ±4°, stronger ±11° in bursts.
- **Cracks**: the kit's levels light, medium, heavy; on the heavier ones a pixel of the Baby's own body colour shows
  beside each crack line.
- **Dev view**: the egg sheet in `baby-lab.html` and `examples/m11d3/eggs-24@3x.png`, `eggs-48@2x.png` (six genomes,
  every crack level, rocking angles, the Baby and the Adult).

## 8. The first birth cinematic (IMPLEMENTED; a local presentation)

For a genuinely new (local, unborn) Blobbi, a new island's intro becomes:

1. the Carrier emerges; 2. the camera introduces the island (both as before); 3. the arrival begins; 4. **the egg
materialises** by the teleport's own falling particles; 5. it rocks gently; 6. harder, with its first crack, then two
more; 7. **it hatches**: a burst of the teleport's own rising pixels from the shell; 8. the Baby is there, facing you,
with a small hop; 9. it looks one way; 10. the other; 11. it faces you; 12. "hello!" with a happy hop; 13. play.

About 37 s (reduced motion about 21.5 s: no rocking, the cracks and the hatch only). Skippable at any moment (the
Baby is simply present, never an egg left behind). The egg never looks around; only the Baby does. The birth plays
exactly once per local Blobbi (`born` in its record, set when the intro ends or is skipped; a reload during it plays
it again). Continuing an island never replays any intro.

**Lifecycle boundary**: the egg and the hatch are a drawing (`live.egg`, presentation only). No canonical stage is
stated, changed, simulated or published; the local Blobbi is a Baby in this game only.

## 9. Existing Blobbi introductions and visit timing (IMPLEMENTED)

- A new island with an existing Blobbi (an Adult, a Baby, a world form): the usual intro, the Blobbi brought in by the
  teleport (Baby-sized particles since M11D.2), looking around, facing you, "hello!". No egg, no rebirth.
- A canonical **egg** still cannot be chosen (an egg does not walk; its hatch is the lifecycle flow of its own app).
- **Visit timing**: a Blobbi's first visit to an island plays the full teleport (a swap about 6.2 s, an arrival 3.6 s);
  later visits half the time (`QUICK_PACE` 0.5: a swap about 3.1 s, an arrival 1.8 s). Remembered per island and
  Blobbi in `blobbi-pixel-lab:island-visits` (local only, never published, never in a save; a damaged record just
  makes the next visit a first one). The swap's rules from M11D.2 hold (faces the player, one Blobbi, actions locked).
- **Future registration**: when a guest Blobbi is registered it remains the same character (same look by
  construction, see the guest plan): no departure or arrival is played for it. Not implemented (no registration yet).

## 10. Canonical lifecycle and identity boundaries

| Never | Where it is enforced |
|---|---|
| Publish or sign anything | no new network code; the M11B boundary test; a source scan in `tests/m11d3.test.ts` |
| Make a private key | no key code; a local Blobbi's id is 8 random bytes used as a name |
| Change a canonical stage | the egg and hatch are presentation (`live.egg`), never written anywhere |
| Change canonical identity | local Blobbis are a separate kind of seed; V3 addresses and world forms unchanged |
| Tie islands to accounts or Blobbis | island saves never name either (unchanged); visits are local memory |
