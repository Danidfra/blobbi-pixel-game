## 1. World forms for V1/V2 (IMPLEMENTED)

A V1 or V2 Blobbi visits Blobbi Pixel in a **temporary, local, V3-style pixel form** (`src/blobbi/world-form.ts`).

| Kept from its event | Derived, deterministic (from its canonical address) |
|---|---|
| base, secondary and eye colour (the kit's own reading, `parseBlobbiEvent(...).visualTraits`, drawn with the kit's Adult V2 colour rule, `mapping: 'kit'`) | body shape (morphology genes) |
| pattern (solid, spotted, striped, gradient) | where its markings sit |
| special mark (star, heart, sparkle; `blush` becomes the procedural freckles, its nearest marking; none) | antenna, horns, ears, tail, belly (V1/V2 art has none of these words) |
| name, life stage (Baby → the pixel Baby body, Adult → the pixel Adult body), owner, `d`, generation | |

- **Seed**: `blobbi-pixel:world-form:v1:<owner pubkey>:<d>`, given to the kit's own `generateGenome` with the stated
  colours, pattern and mark. The same Blobbi always gets the same form on every device; two Blobbis never share one
  by chance; never a random genome. The string is not 64 hex digits, so it can never be read as a V3 seed, and no V3
  address derives it. `WORLD_FORM_VERSION = 1`: another mapping would be a new version, never a silent change.
- **Unknown words** (a pattern or mark this version does not know) read as solid / none; unreadable colours mean no
  world form (the Blobbi stays listed with its reason).
- **Identity guarantees**: the source is `{ kind: 'world-form', pubkey, d, generation, seed, traits }`; its identity
  is still `31124:<pubkey>:<d>` and its generation still V1/V2. The event is only read (a frozen event passes through
  unchanged, tested); nothing is signed or published; other apps never see the form.

## 2. World form UX (IMPLEMENTED)

- MY BLOBBIS lists V1/V2 Blobbis with their world-form picture; the detail shows the form and the original (a V1/V2
  Baby: the kit's baby art; an Adult: its three colours, V1/V2 adult art is not rasterized) and "Visits in its world
  form: a different look for this island only. Its original form stays exactly the same."
- The first time a given V1/V2 Blobbi is chosen on this device, the briefed explanation replaces the detail:
  "Your Blobbi discovered a new form!", the body text, both pictures ("Original", "In this world"), TRY THIS FORM
  (Enter) and NOT NOW (Backspace, N, or the button; Escape closes the screen). TRY THIS FORM remembers it for that
  Blobbi (`blobbi-pixel-lab:world-forms`, a tiny per-browser record) and brings it; later choices go straight in.
  NOT NOW changes nothing and remembers nothing. EN, PT-BR, ES (the brief's copy), no em dash.

## 3. V1/V2 visits (IMPLEMENTED)

V1/V2 Babies and Adults with readable colours are `available` (eggs never: an egg does not walk). Choosing stays
explicit; only the connected account's own Blobbi can be chosen (the chooser checks the owner); the selection record
stores the world-form source **only beside its account**, re-derives the seed on every load and refuses the whole
record on any mismatch (a tampered seed, look, owner or a missing account). Disconnect: the visitor walks; the same
account again: the world form comes back; another account: not theirs. Connecting never replaces the walking Blobbi.
Older code (M11D.1) does not know the kind and ignores the record (the visitor walks), never another Blobbi.

## 4. Departure and arrival (IMPLEMENTED)

`LiveWorld.swapBlobbi` uses the existing teleport (`src/game/teleport.ts`), no new visual language:

1. the outgoing Blobbi stops (swing, fishing, placing and build modes end) and turns to face the player (front view);
2. it plays the teleport departure (concentrates, dissolves from the top into pixels rising to the sky);
3. when it is gone, the incoming Blobbi takes **the same spot** (`setBlobbi` keeps x, y), faces the player;
4. it plays the teleport arrival (pixels fall, it rebuilds from the bottom, opens its eyes, smiles);
5. play resumes. About 6.2 s in all (reduced motion: the teleport's own short form).

Movement, actions and autonomy are locked throughout (the teleport's own lock: pressing keys or E does nothing). There
is only ever one Blobbi entity. **Rapid choices**: while one is leaving, a new choice only changes who arrives;
choosing the one already walking turns its departure back into an arrival; a choice during an arrival turns it into a
departure, then the newest one arrives. A cancelled transition never strands anyone (the chosen Blobbi is present).
**Immediate** (no effect) when the effect cannot play: already away, inside a home, aboard the canoe, in bed, before
play, on the title or during the intro. Presence is never saved: a reload mid-swap shows the chosen Blobbi, present.
Choices from MY BLOBBIS are made with the world paused (menus pause it), so the swap plays as the menu closes.

## 5. Baby particle effects (IMPLEMENTED)

`LiveWorld.bodyH` / `bodyK`: the body as drawn (a Baby's own height, `babyHeightFor`, and its ratio to the adult: 1
for adults). Every Blobbi-anchored particle (pet hearts, food dust, sparkles, notes, the "!" and the Zzz) rises from
`bodyH` and spreads over `bodyK` times the adult spread; adult numbers are identical (k = 1, the same random draws).
The teleport is sized to the Blobbi actually drawn: adult 16 × 7 as before; a Baby its drawn height and the half width
of its droplet (`TeleportController.resize`, set on every Blobbi change, before a swap's arrival begins). Readability
at every zoom comes from the teleport renderer's existing per-zoom pixel size.

## 6. Baby tool attachment (IMPLEMENTED)

Both renderers draw a tool that is **in front** one world pixel to the right (the approved M2.3 nudge): one native
pixel at 1×, but 2, 4 and 6 native pixels at exploration, close range and up close, so a Baby's tool floated beside it
at higher zooms. A Baby's grip now takes that nudge back (`round(size / 12)` native pixels) for tools drawn in front,
including the back-view swing that comes in front, so every drawn grip **touches the body edge from outside**: never on
the body (no clipping), never away from it, at 12, 16, 24, 48 and 72 px, for the axe, pickaxe, hoe, watering can and
rod (tested). No arms, the face clear (from the side the tool still hangs at the back edge), the action animations and
the far-hand rule unchanged. Adults unchanged.

## 8. Save compatibility

Island saves are unchanged (schema 12; they never name a Blobbi: tested byte for byte around a swap to a world form).
No saved guest character exists today (every guest walks with the demo `blobbi-9`), so none can be overwritten. New
per-browser record: `blobbi-pixel-lab:world-forms`. The selection record stays version 1 (a new source kind).

## 10. Known limitations

- **KI-81** The V1/V2 `size` tag is not used by world forms.
- **KI-82** A V1/V2 Adult's original is shown as its colours only (its authored art is not rasterized).
- **KI-83** The HUD shows the incoming Blobbi's name as soon as it is chosen, while the outgoing one is still leaving.
- **KI-84** A swap takes about 6 s (the full teleport); the owner may prefer a shorter, swap-only timing.
- **KI-85** World-form anatomy traits (horns, ears...) come from the address-derived seed: a V1/V2 Blobbi may show
  horns its original art never had (the owner's chosen rule; reviewed on four looks only).
- KI-76 (V1/V2 Babies cannot visit) is resolved by world forms.
