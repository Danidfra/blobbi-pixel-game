## What M9 changes

1. **Rest** ([gameplay systems](../game-design/GAMEPLAY-SYSTEMS.md#care-and-friendship)): R outside walks home,
   enters by the door and climbs into the nearest reachable bed; inside, straight into bed; tucked under the blanket
   (bed behind, blanket in front, canonical Blobbi with closed eyes); climbs out when woken. Handles no house, no
   bed, unreachable homes, moved beds, interruption, several beds and reloads.
2. **Turtle flippers** emerge from under the shell (they were drawn over its rim): the paddling flippers are masked
   by the body, the static underlay draws them first. Variant B, Variant S and grown islands.
3. **Home styles** ([housing](../specifications/HOUSING.md#unlocks)): the cause was not broken checks but a generous
   base set, previews that drew locked styles like open ones, and T cycling floors and walls with no check. Now one
   style per part to start; the rest earned through quests, discoveries, crafting, gathering, farming, growth and
   upgrades; padlocked previews; notices when earned; styles already in a save are kept.
4. **Growth** ([world growth](../specifications/WORLD-GROWTH.md)): four stages with rising, varied requirements, and
   stage 4 — the Sanctuary — over most of each Carrier's suitable surface, adding land only, anatomy kept clear,
   growth version 3 with stages 1–3 unchanged.
5. **World feel:** smoking chimneys and glowing windows on grown homes, a stronger night glow, a notice per growth
   requirement met, the in-bed prompt.
