## 4. Progression and achievements

Quests, chapters, recipes, house levels, growth stages, the Carrier bond, friendship, discoveries and the fish log are
**world snapshot** state (private, inside the island event). None is published as its own event. Candidates for a
*later*, public, player-signed journal that another app could show (on a profile or a Blobbi page): "Island grew to
stage 4", "Built the Dream Home", "Caught every fish", "Found all sea finds" — at most a handful, as claims not
proofs. No badge protocol is in use in the ecosystem (NIP-58 unused; Blobbi Island's `achievements` profile tag is
written through but never granted), so this waits for an ecosystem decision.

## 5. Privacy

| Data | Recommendation | Why / UX consequence |
|---|---|---|
| The fact that a player uses Blobbi Pixel | public (the event exists) | unavoidable with relays; acceptable |
| Island name, species, growth stage | private in the alpha; public only in a later opt-in world card | lets visiting come later without leaking now |
| World seed / identity | private | a seed reveals the island's whole layout |
| Buildings, inventory, quests, friendship, discoveries | private (NIP-44 to self) | progress is personal; encryption means relays cannot index it, which is fine for a save |
| Activity timestamps | `created_at` is public; publish at most every few minutes, rounded | coarse play times visible: say so in the account screen |
| Player position, settings, device data | local only | no benefit to sharing |
| Farm spends (M11E) | public (the protocol is public) | the player sees "Used in Blobbi Pixel" in the Farm |

## 6. Answers to the research questions

1. **Same event for identity and state?** No final split yet: the alpha uses **one private event per island** (state
   includes the generation inputs); a **separate public card** is added only when visiting needs it.
2. **Addressable events?** Yes: kind 30078 (NIP-78), `d` = `blobbi-pixel/island/<islandId>`.
3. **Stable island id?** A new opaque 128-bit `islandId` (schema 13), made once; the generation identity string stays
   the local key and travels inside the state.
4. **Multiple islands?** One addressable event per island plus an index event listing them and the active one.
5. **Guest claim?** On connect: no remote islands → offer to save this one; a guest island is bound to the pubkey only
   after the player confirms.
6. **Account already has an island?** Show both; *keep both* (adds the guest island to the account), *open the saved
   one here*, or *not now*. Never merge, never overwrite.
7. **Not overwriting newer local state?** Compare `revision` (+ `worldTime`); older remote never replaces local;
   adopting remote only when local is unchanged since the last sync, after a local backup; both changed → ask.
8. **Minimal relay strategy?** Two ecosystem relays (ditto, primal) + the player's own; write to all, ≥ 1 ack; newest
   valid wins; publish on milestones and at most every 5 minutes of play.
9. **Offline?** Play continues; changes are marked unsynced and published later.
10. **Lost relay data?** The device's record republishes it; losing device and relays loses the island (stated).
11. **Canonical Blobbi fields?** The address `31124:<owner>:<d>`; for V3 the seed derived from it
    (`deriveBlobbiV3Seed`) and the renderer's Algorithm 1; `stage`, `state`, `name` from the event. Everything else
    is app-specific.
12. **Import without duplicates?** Yes: Pixel only reads 31124 and stores the address; it never creates or republishes
    a Blobbi.
13. **Interoperable items?** Realistically now: Nostr Farm produce (consumed). Later: fish and sea finds as issuer
    definitions once a consumer commits.
14. **Inventory operations supported?** Definitions, inventories (snapshots), placements, spends, folds. Not grants,
    transfers, reservations, crafting.
15. **What needs a trusted issuer?** Anything another app should believe: item definitions, and any future earned or
    valuable item (and grants, which are unspecified).
16. **World progress representation?** A full private snapshot of the island record per island (not deltas).
17. **What stays local?** Settings, camera, position, device pointers, backups, `local:` player ids.
18. **Snapshot sizes?** 1.5 KB new, 9.2 KB after the full story, 39 KB heavy synthetic (0.7 / 2.8 / 6 KB gzip).
19. **Libraries to reuse?** `@nostrify/nostrify` (framework-free signers and pools), `@blobbi-kit/core` (validation,
    derivation; already a dependency), `@nostr-games/inventory` (state, spends). Not `@blobbi-kit/react` or `NLogin`
    (React).
20. **Safest first step?** M11B: read-only signer connection with zero writes, enforced by tests.

## 8. Highest-risk decisions

1. **Guest claiming and conflict rules** (M11D): a mistake here loses a player's island. Mitigation: a pure decision
   function, exhaustive tests, a local backup before any replacement.
2. **Kind 30078 vs a dedicated kind**: 30078 is draft and "not for interoperability"; moving later means a migration
   of every published island.
3. **NIP-44 availability** across signers: without it, sync must stay off or go public.
4. **Relying on partner apps** (Farm) whose branches are moving (uncommitted seed-shop work).
