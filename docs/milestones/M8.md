## What M8 adds

1. **First-time player flow** (`src/ui/title.ts`, `src/playground/main.ts`): with no saved island the title opens on
   WELCOME → BEGIN → CHOOSE YOUR WORLD CARRIER (three animated cards: Sea Turtle, Manta Ray, Great Whale, each with a
   personality line; the choice changes look and mood, never difficulty) → NAME YOUR ISLAND (optional; keyboard, or
   a phone keyboard via TYPE A NAME; SURPRISE ME suggests one) → CREATE ISLAND (taken exactly once). Returning
   players: CONTINUE (the most recently played island), MY ISLANDS (open or delete), NEW ISLAND (the same steps),
   SETTINGS, ABOUT (WATCH THE … INTRO, a non-destructive preview). A plain link opens the game (Play), not the lab.
2. **The intro in play**: the island is generated (no cottage; a small Variant S shell for turtles), saved at once
   with `intro: pending`, and the existing `IntroCinematic` plays on the real world, then hands over to play with
   the cinematic's camera. Skip (key, click, tap) or the natural end mark it `skipped` / `done`. A reload during the
   intro keeps the island and plays it again; a failing frame or start goes on into play; creation failures restore
   the previous world and save nothing. See [intro spec](../specifications/INTRO-CINEMATIC.md).
3. **No house at the start**: new worlds are generated with `generatedHouse: false` (identity `|house=none`): no
   procedural cottage, fence, garden or path. That cottage was scenery (not enterable); the enterable home has always
   been the player-built shelter. Older worlds keep their cottage, byte for byte.
4. **The first home** ([quests](../specifications/QUESTS.md)): chapter 1 is now meet the Blobbi → explore → gather
   → the axe → wood → materials (6 wood, 3 stones) → build → step inside (a potted plant as a gift) → place it.
   Farming is chapter 2, the Carrier chapter 3, house upgrades chapter 4.
5. **Housing** ([spec](../specifications/HOUSING.md)): Cozy Shelter, Cottage, Dream Home — own art, footprints and
   room layouts (one room; nook + main room + tiled kitchen; bedroom + study + kitchen + living room), earned
   upgrades with a confirmation on the HOME panel (1), furniture relocated without loss, customisation with
   progressive unlocks and previews.
6. **Tutorial** ([spec](../specifications/TUTORIAL.md)): 14 contextual tips, one at a time, done by doing, skippable
   and saved per island.
7. **Player guide** (? or F1; [pages](../game-design/PLAYER-GUIDE.md)).
8. **Mobile**: full-screen game on small screens, rotate hint in portrait, touch mode on the first touch anywhere
   (bigger menu bar without key names, wrapped on narrow screens), a mode bar for build/place/move, the prompt and
   tips above the menu bar.
9. **Save deletion safety** (KI-01 fixed): confirmations that name the island and refuse a too-quick answer.
10. **Save schema 10** ([save docs](../architecture/SAVE-AND-PERSISTENCE.md)): name, intro, tutorial, house level,
    look and upgrade time, wall finish. Records 1–9 migrate (tested for all three Carriers).

The opening experience is described minute by minute in [the first 30 minutes](../game-design/FIRST-30-MINUTES.md).
