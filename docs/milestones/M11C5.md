Key findings (details and evidence in each document):

1. **A V3 look is a function of its owner**: seed = SHA-256(domain, owner pubkey, d), and the canonical d embeds the
   pubkey prefix; a V3 event can carry no appearance. A random local seed can never become that look. To keep a guest
   Blobbi's look when registered, its owner key must exist at birth (nostr-pet's guest mode does exactly this), or
   the look changes at registration (shown before signing), or the kit's model changes.
2. **The kit draws V3 babies** from the same genome (0.64 scale, droplet body, simple eyes, hop gait, **no arms or
   feet**, budding traits); identity is the same across egg, baby and adult. V2 has no baby art (it draws V1's). The
   pixel renderer is adult-only, and its tool system finds a hand by rasterizing the arm: babies need a new carry.
3. **"4 vs 10 segments" is display**, not capacity: stats are 1-100 for every stage; babies decay faster
   (hunger -8/h vs -5/h). Recommended: keep 0-100 needs, draw 4 or 10 segments, and express "smaller capacity" only in
   a game-local work-energy pool (baby 40, adult 100).
4. **Energy**: Pixel's energy drains passively (more while walking) and no action costs anything. Proposed: a separate
   work energy where walking, exploring, picking up, petting and feeding are free; chopping/mining/tilling 2 per hit,
   watering/casting/cooking 1, building 8/2/1; sleep restores all, naps, food and play help; never death or penalties.
5. **Accounts**: NIP-46 first (no player secret in the game); "create one here" only with a forced, encrypted (NIP-49)
   backup and honest wording; browser storage is never presented as recovery; nsec import not offered by default.
6. **Registration** must be idempotent: d fixed before signing, preflight by d, the signed event stored locally before
   publishing and republished unchanged on retry, partial publication completed later, never overwriting a newer state.

## Decisions requiring the owner's approval

1. Guest Blobbi identity: reserved owner key at birth (A, like nostr-pet; needs secret storage) or a local Blobbi that
   changes look at registration (B)?
2. Secret keys in the browser at all (create-in-game, guest key reservation)? NIP-46 now?
3. The registered Baby's event shape (kit hatch shape with `evolving` and missions, or `none`) and publish relays.
4. The baby tool carry style and minimum on-screen size; whether eggs appear (recommended: only as a hatching moment).
5. The work-energy costs table and pools; exhaustion refuses or slows.
6. The invitation's numbers (10 minutes, 3 quests, 3-day cooldown, 90 s on screen) after the owner plays.

## Recommended implementation order

1. Owner playtest of M11C and this invitation. 2. Baby sprite sheets for review, then stage-aware pixel rendering and
the baby carry (no gameplay change). 3. NIP-46. 4. Work energy (schema 13 with an explicit migration). 5. Account
creation with backup. 6. Guest Baby birth and registration with a fake relay, then real publishing behind an explicit
button.
