## 2. Selection model audit (before any change)

| Question | Finding |
|---|---|
| 1. An explicit local selection timestamp? | **No.** The selection record (`blobbi-pixel-lab:selected-blobbi`, `src/game/blobbi-selection.ts`) held `version`, `source`, and optionally `name`, `account`, `stage`, `guest`. No time, no origin. |
| 2. Explicit selection vs automatic restoration? | **Partly, in code paths only.** `selectBlobbi` / `storeSelection` write the record (MY BLOBBIS, the visiting Blobbi, the lab's chooser, and the host's BRING / welcome adoption); `applyBlobbi` alone walks without writing (`syncAccountBlobbi` on load, connect, disconnect). The record itself could not tell a player's act from the game's adoption. |
| 3. The last Blobbi that visited each island? | **Yes.** `blobbi-pixel-lab:island-visits` (`src/game/island-visits.ts`): per island, `last` (seed, name, stage) and `seen` (up to 40 seeds). Presentation memory only. |
| 4. Does changing the walking Blobbi alter the island save? | **No.** `WorldSave` is owned by the local world (`owner: 'local-world'`) and never names a Blobbi; the selection and the visits are separate records. |
| 5. Does it overwrite the previous association? | `last` is replaced by the newest visitor; `seen` keeps every earlier one (up to 40). |
| 6. Can several Blobbis visit the same island? | **Yes**, by design (M11D.3): an island is never owned by a Blobbi. |
| 7. A remembered choice to ignore the global companion? | **Yes**: `blobbi-pixel-lab:entry-choice` (`rememberStay` / `stayedWith`, `src/game/world-entry.ts`): per island, `{ pubkey, d, at }` for CONTINUE WITH. |
| 8. How is it invalidated? | Only when the current companion's `d` (or the identity) changes. A re-selection of the SAME Blobbi elsewhere, later, did not count. |
| 9. If the global companion changes again? | The question is asked again (the stored `d` no longer matches). |
| 10. Enough reliable information to compare? | **No.** The remote side had a time (the profile event's `created_at`, seconds, already carried as `profileAt` by the resolver); the local side had none. |

Timestamps that exist but are NOT selection times, and are not used: the world save's `worldTime` and the index's
last-played time (play, not choice), `island-visits` (no time at all), the guest record's `createdAt` / `bornAt` /
`activatedAt` (birth and sharing), a kind 31124 `created_at` (the Blobbi's state).

## 3. Local versus global selection

- **Canonical Blobbi state** (kind 31124): what a Blobbi is and how it is. Its `created_at` never decides who walks.
- **Global current companion** (kind 11125 `current_companion`): the Blobbi chosen across the Blobbi apps; the
  event's `created_at` is when that choice was published. Read only (the M11D.8 resolver, unchanged).
- **Local explicit selection**: an act of the player in this game. From M11D.8.3 the selection record carries
  `selectedAt` (ms, this device's clock) and `selectedBy`: `player` for MY BLOBBIS, BRING, CONTINUE WITH on "who
  comes along?", THE VISITING BLOBBI, a newborn, a restore's CONTINUE WITH / BRING, the lab's chooser; `remote` when
  the game adopts the current Blobbi of the other worlds, stamped with THAT choice's own time (the profile's
  `created_at`), never with "now". A reload, an automatic entry, a resume, the account sync, a newer kind 31124 or a
  screen never write the record (`syncAccountBlobbi` only applies; the tests pin this).

## 4. Exact precedence (`decideEntry`, `src/game/world-entry.ts`)

Given one island of this identity, the walking Blobbi W (the device's choice) and a current Blobbi C that can walk:

1. C is W: enter. Nothing is compared.
2. Otherwise `compareSelections(local.at, C.at, now)`:
   - `remote` (C's time is later than the local stamp by more than 60 s, both in the past of this device's clock plus
     60 s): **`bring`**: C comes along, no question; the record becomes C, `selectedBy: 'remote'`, `selectedAt` = C's time.
   - `local` (the local stamp is later by more than 60 s): **enter with W**, no question.
   - `ambiguous` (no local stamp, no remote time, the two within 60 s of each other, either beyond now + 60 s, or no
     clock): the remembered answer (`stayedWith`) if any, else **"who comes along?"** as before. BRING stamps the
     choice as the player's, now; CONTINUE WITH remembers the stay AND stamps the walking Blobbi as the player's, now.
3. The rest is unchanged: several islands keep the islands page; no island offers WELCOME BACK with C (adopted,
   `remote`); an egg or a Blobbi that cannot walk never comes along; offline (no answer) enters with W; `#menu` and
   SETTINGS > MAIN MENU keep the title; an unverified extension never enters.

The brief's examples, as tested (`tests/m11d8-3-selection.test.ts`): A remote newer: Moon comes along. B local newer:
Boca rosa stays. C a newer kind 31124 for Boca rosa: irrelevant (the resolver's `profileAt` is the profile's). D a
reload after an adoption: same Blobbi, no new stamp. E Moon chosen again elsewhere, later: Moon again.

## 5. Timestamp handling and limitations

- Local stamps are this device's clock in ms; remote times are the author's `created_at` in seconds, converted once
  (`currentSelectionAt`). Non-finite, zero or negative values read as "no time".
- `SELECTION_SKEW_MS` = 60 s: two choices closer than that cannot be ordered (clocks of different devices), and a
  time beyond now + 60 s (a future-dated event, a device clock far ahead) is not trusted. Equal times are ambiguous.
- No cryptographic ordering exists across devices: `created_at` is author-supplied. The rule is deliberately plain and
  the safe question remains for every unclear case. A wrong clock on one device can at worst bring the question back,
  or prefer the wrong Blobbi for one entry, which the player corrects with one explicit choice (stamped newer).
- Stale cached events: an OLDER remote choice than the one already adopted reads as `local` (the adoption carries the
  newer remote time): nothing flips back.
- Offline: no remote time; the device's choice walks, nothing is stamped.

## 6. Migration for existing saves

Records written before M11D.8.3 have no stamp. They are never backdated and never treated as older than a remote
choice: the comparison is ambiguous, so "who comes along?" is asked once, as in M11D.8, and the answer stamps the
record. From then on the rule applies. Older code ignores the two new fields; every other field is unchanged, and
islands, visits, guest records and the account record are untouched.

## 7. Island visitor semantics (unchanged, verified)

The island save (terrain, buildings, resources, inventory, plots, rooms, containers, quests, growth) is the world's
and never names a Blobbi; a visitor change writes only the visits record (`last` replaced, `seen` extended) and the
selection record. No ownership or access model exists or was added; nothing is inferred from a Blobbi event. The
browser check compares the save's buildings, bag, plots, rooms and containers before and after Moon came along.

## 8. Offline behaviour

Unchanged: the relays get 4 s; with no answer the island opens with the device's choice. Nothing is stamped, nothing
is remembered, and the next online load compares as usual.

## 9. The loading page

Shorter words: "Your Blobbi is on the way!" / "Finding your Blobbi across connected worlds..."; "Seu Blobbi está
chegando!" / "Procurando seu Blobbi em outros mundos..."; "¡Tu Blobbi ya viene!" / "Buscando a tu Blobbi en otros
mundos...". The words now sit on a dark translucent panel (the island darkened to 70% night blue under a one-pixel
frame; no blur, no new motion), and the slow or extension line on a second small panel. Everything else is as in
M11D.8.2: no minimum time, no menu under a pending entry, MAIN MENU after 1.5 s, ESC, the give-up, reduced motion,
every language at both text sizes.

## 10. Future synchronisation (not built)

A choice made in Pixel stays on this device: other apps will not know it until an explicit, consented publication of
kind 11125 exists (a SHARE-like step with the account's signer). Until then the asymmetry is: Ditto's and Standalone's
Set as companion reach Pixel; Pixel's choice reaches nobody. Documented in CURRENT-BLOBBI §5.

## 11. Manual validation checklist (disposable identities only)

1. **Remote newer.** Boca rosa walking on an island; in Ditto, Set as companion on Moon; reload Pixel: the loading
   page, then Moon arrives on the island, no question. SETTINGS > ACCOUNT > MY BLOBBIS shows Moon as the one here.
2. **Local newer.** In MY BLOBBIS, BRING Boca rosa; reload: Boca rosa, no question (Moon still current in Ditto).
3. **Remote again.** In Ditto, unset and set Moon again (a new, later 11125); reload: Moon again, no question.
4. **Same choice.** Nothing changed on either side; reload: straight in.
5. **Old record.** Restore a backup made before M11D.8.3 (or clear the stamp in DevTools): the question once; answer
   CONTINUE WITH; reload: no question; set the companion again in Ditto: Moon comes along.
6. **Island untouched.** Buildings, bag, plots and rooms identical after every visitor change.
7. **Offline.** Network off: the device's choice, no question.
8. **Egg.** Set an egg as companion in Ditto: nothing comes along; the walking Blobbi stays.
9. **Extension.** Switch accounts in the extension: the title; no island opens.
10. **Nothing sent.** No EVENT on any load (the extension never asks to sign).

## 14. Limitations

- One device clock orders both sides; see §5. The 60 s window is a judgement, not a measurement.
- Pixel's choice is not shared (§10).
- A profile without a usable time (none seen in practice; `created_at` is mandatory) falls back to the question.
